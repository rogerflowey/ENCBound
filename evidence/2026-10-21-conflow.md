## Reproduction and limits

```sh
python3 apps/conflow/run.py --trace
```

Build/run/debugger logs, per-unit manifests and full results remain under
`build/conflow`. The checked-in JSON captures the final matrix and source/image
identities. Per-profile Make commands and standalone native/trace commands are
available in the scripts; the README records SDK/tool dependencies and paths.

No ENCBound change blocker remains for these eight ECALLs. Original `user_check`
arrays are host memory; the traces establish that the selected workload also
executes checks on enclave stack and heap storage. The checks do not supply
logical tensor shape validation or host-buffer length guarantees.

Positive bounded consistent dimensions and finite values are the tested domain.
Malformed dimensions, signed dimension overflow, allocation exhaustion,
NaN/Inf and comprehensive numerical accuracy remain unestablished. The full
TensorFlow/Python build, DeepCrossing model, additional operators, concurrency,
hardware, performance, application vulnerability prevention and cryptographic
security of the sharing scheme are not claimed. Existing gates and frozen
wallet/LZ4 target decisions are unchanged.
